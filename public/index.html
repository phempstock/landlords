    What the map shows

    HMOs (houses in multiple occupation) are homes shared by people from more than one household. Larger ones need a mandatory licence; since 2024 smaller ones across Nottingham need an additional licence.

    Selective licences cover most other privately rented homes in the parts of the city where the council requires them.

    Applications are licences applied for and not yet granted; some are renewals of a licence the property already holds.

    Only licensed properties and pending applications appear. A home that should be licensed but isn’t won’t be on the map.

    Pins show where the council has placed each property; positions are approximate. The council’s registers are the official record.
